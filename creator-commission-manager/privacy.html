Creator Commission Manager Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Creator Commission Manager is a Discord bot designed to help creators organize commission queues, customer requests, deadlines, payments, notes, and commission workflow.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the bot collects, why it is collected, how it is used, and when it is deleted.

Information We Collect

Creator Commission Manager stores only information needed to operate its commission-management features.

Discord Account and Server Information

The bot may store:

  • Discord server IDs
  • Discord user IDs for creators and customers
  • Discord role IDs used for commission-management permissions
  • Discord channel IDs
  • Discord message IDs associated with commission panels

These identifiers allow the bot to associate commission queues, requests, panels, settings, and permissions with the correct Discord server and users.

Commission Information

When the bot is used to manage commissions, it may store:

  • Commission request descriptions submitted by customers
  • Commission queue names and descriptions
  • Commission status
  • Commission deadlines
  • Delivery status and delivery timestamps
  • Reasons a commission was declined
  • Reasons a commission is waiting on the customer
  • Private notes entered by the creator
  • Commission prices
  • Currency information
  • Payment status

Creator Commission Manager does not process payments or collect payment-card or banking information. Payment fields are used only for tracking information manually entered into the bot.

Creator Settings

The bot may store settings chosen by creators, including:

  • Timezone
  • Commission queue settings
  • Queue availability and slot limits
  • Archived queue status

Timezone information is used to calculate deadlines and reminders.

Server Settings

The bot may store server-specific configuration, including:

  • Who is permitted to manage commissions
  • A selected Discord role used for commission access

How We Use Information

Information stored by Creator Commission Manager is used only to provide the bot's functionality, including:

  • Creating and managing commission queues
  • Accepting customer commission requests
  • Tracking commission progress
  • Managing deadlines
  • Sending deadline reminders
  • Displaying commission panels
  • Tracking manually entered payment information
  • Saving creator notes
  • Enforcing server commission-management permissions
  • Restoring commission information across bot restarts

The bot does not use commission information for advertising or profiling.

Private Creator Notes

Creators may save private notes about commission requests. These notes are intended for the creator managing the commission and are not displayed to customers through normal bot functionality.

Creators should avoid entering unnecessary sensitive personal information into private notes.

Payment Information

Creator Commission Manager may store a commission price and payment status such as Unpaid, Deposit Paid, Partially Paid, or Paid in Full.

These fields are organizational tools only. Creator Commission Manager does not process payments, store credit or debit card numbers, store bank-account information, or connect directly to a payment processor.

Data Retention

Commission information is retained while the bot remains installed in a Discord server so that queues, requests, history, and settings continue to function.

When Creator Commission Manager is removed from a Discord server, the bot automatically deletes the commission data associated with that server.

This includes associated:

  • Commission queues
  • Commission requests and request history
  • Creator notes
  • Deadlines
  • Reminder records
  • Commission panel records
  • Creator timezone settings
  • Price and payment-status information
  • Server commission-access settings

Because this deletion is automatic, reinstalling the bot after it has been removed will not restore the previous server's commission data.

Data Deletion Requests

If you believe Creator Commission Manager is storing information associated with you and would like to request its deletion, contact:

ravenberries@proton.me

Please include enough information to identify the relevant Discord server or bot data. Do not send passwords, authentication tokens, payment-card information, or other sensitive credentials.

Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

  • Discord passwords
  • Discord authentication tokens
  • Credit or debit card numbers
  • Bank-account credentials
  • Government identification numbers
  • General Discord message history
  • Messages from channels unrelated to the bot's features

The bot primarily receives information users deliberately submit through its commands, buttons, and forms.

Discord

Creator Commission Manager operates through Discord and therefore depends on Discord's services.

Discord may independently collect and process information according to its own terms and privacy practices. Creator Commission Manager does not control Discord's independent data practices.

Service Providers

Infrastructure providers may process stored information as necessary to host and operate Creator Commission Manager.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to limit access to the bot's stored information and to prevent unauthorized access or modification. However, no online service or database can guarantee absolute security.

Users should avoid submitting highly sensitive personal information through commission requests or creator notes.

Children's Privacy

Creator Commission Manager is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children below the minimum age permitted to use Discord in their jurisdiction.

If you believe information has been collected from a child inappropriately, contact ravenberries@proton.me .

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Creator Commission Manager changes its features, data practices, or infrastructure. The date at the top of this policy will be updated when material changes are made.
