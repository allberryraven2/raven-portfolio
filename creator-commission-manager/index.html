Creator Commission Manager

Commission queues and workflow management inside Discord.

Built for artists, crafters, and other creators who want to manage requests without moving the entire commission process into another app. Create queues, accept customer requests, track progress, manage deadlines, record payment status, and keep creator notes in one place.

Features

The bot handles the commission workflow from an open queue through delivery.

queues.exe □ ─ ×

Commission queues

Create multiple queues with their own descriptions, slot limits, and availability. Open, close, edit, archive, restore, or delete queues as needed.

workflow.exe □ ─ ×

Request workflow

Customers submit requests from a public panel. Creators can accept or reject, start work, wait on the customer, resume, mark work ready, deliver it, and finish the commission after customer confirmation.

deadlines.ini □ ─ ×

Deadlines and reminders

Set commission deadlines and a creator timezone. Automatic reminders help surface upcoming and overdue work without requiring a separate calendar.

tracking.ini □ ─ ×

Price and payment tracking

Record commission prices and payment status, including unpaid, deposit paid, partially paid, and paid in full. The bot tracks status only and does not process payments.

notes.txt □ ─ ×

Creator notes

Keep private notes attached to commission requests for internal reference while the rest of the workflow stays organized in Discord.

access.cfg □ ─ ×

Server access controls

Server owners can allow commission management for everyone, restrict it to the server owner, or require a selected Discord role. Customer request actions remain separate from creator management access.

commission.exe /help

Core Commands

Everything is organized under the /commission command.

commands.txt □ ─ ×
/commission help Show the bot's built-in help and workflow information.
/commission setup Create a new commission queue.
/commission list View your current non-archived commission queues.
/commission panel Post a public commission panel customers can use to submit requests.
/commission open / close Control whether a queue is currently accepting requests.
/commission edit Update a queue's name, description, or slot limit.
/commission archive / restore Move completed queues out of the active list and restore them later.
/commission access Let the server owner view or change who can use commission-management tools.

data_and_privacy.txt

Data & Privacy

privacy_summary.txt □ ─ ×

Creator Commission Manager stores the Discord identifiers and commission data needed to run its queues and workflow. Payment information is limited to the price and payment status entered into the bot. It does not collect card, banking, or payment-account credentials and does not process payments.

When the bot is removed from a Discord server, data associated with that server is automatically deleted from the bot's database.
